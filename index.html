Tips for PDFs

  • Use a PDF with selectable text (exported from Excel, Word or accounting software). Scanned images can't be read.
  • Years should appear in a header row, e.g. "2024 2023 2022".
  • If figures are "in thousands" or "in millions", the app detects it, or you can set it under Figures are in.
